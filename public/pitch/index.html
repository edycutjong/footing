Dashed boxes are plain code, solid boxes are models. Nano answers a yes-or-no question on the laptop. A "no passage" is settled there. A "supports" always goes to the cloud for full versus partial. PDFs and JavaScript pages go straight to the cloud, because only the cloud can read them.

Why not one model?

Nano alone

21/30 on the three-way verdict. Under our 24/30 bar.

Cloud alone

All 20 held-out claims go up. With Nano: 19.

Both

Nano reads readable pages locally; the cloud decides every “supports”.

Footing · v1.1.0 · kill test 2026-11-21, Chrome 157, M1 Max · evidence/p0c/ · src/lib/route/router.ts06 / 11
We measured before we split. On the frozen thirty claims Nano got 21 right at telling full support from partial, under our bar of 24, so it never makes that call. The cloud alone would spend quota on every citation. On the 20 held-out claims Nano kept 1 off the cloud, 8 of the frozen 30: a small saving, and we say so. So Nano triages every readable page on the laptop and the cloud makes the final call on anything Nano thinks is supported.
