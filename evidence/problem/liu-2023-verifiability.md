# Problem size: how often a cited link supports its sentence

Source: Nelson F. Liu, Tianyi Zhang, Percy Liang. "Evaluating Verifiability in Generative Search Engines."
arXiv:2304.09848 (v2, 23 Oct 2023; Findings of EMNLP 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09848
Opened 2026-11-22 (HTTP 200, v2 abstract).

Quoted from the abstract:
- "We conduct human evaluation to audit four popular generative search engines."
- "a mere 51.5% of generated sentences are fully supported by citations"
- "only 74.5% of citations support their associated sentence"

Used in: README.md (The Problem) and the Devpost text (Inspiration). This is a 2023 study of other products, not a
measurement of Footing; Footing's own numbers are in evidence/summary.json.
